# Weekly Security Brief — Example NGO Kenya

**Region:** Kenya  
**Week of:** 10–16 November 2026  
**Prepared for:** Security Focal Point

---

## Executive Summary
Overall security environment remained within expected parameters. No major escalations reported in primary operating areas. Routine crime and seasonal factors continue to drive the majority of incidents.

## Crime & Banditry
Public reporting indicates continued livestock-related incidents in traditional hotspot counties. Urban centres continue to experience elevated levels of petty crime and opportunistic theft. Road movement after dark remains the highest practical risk for staff.

## Political Security
No significant political violence recorded. Monitoring of planned public gatherings continues as standard practice.

## Terrorism & Extremism
No new public claims of operational significance during the period. Existing posture and movement controls remain appropriate.

## Health
Standard seasonal health advisories apply. No exceptional public health notifications affecting programme areas.

## Recommendations
1. Maintain current security posture and movement controls.
2. Continue prohibition on non-essential night road travel in high-risk counties.
3. Ensure all staff have updated emergency contact cascade.
4. Review any pending travel requests against the latest county-level assessments.
